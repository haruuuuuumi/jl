JeoLee: a tandem where every little moment becomes a story worth remembering.

JeoLee, the fan-favorite tandem of Jeremiah Emmanuel “Jeo” Ong and Gehlee Dangca, caught viewers’ attention with their shared moments, contrasting personalities, and natural chemistry.

Jeo brings a laid-back charm, an adventurous spirit, and a boy-next-door warmth. Gehlee brings her own charm, an expressive nature, and a playful energy. Together, they make even the smallest moments worth replaying, from teasing and laughter to quiet conversations and times when they simply seem at ease with each other.

Jeo and Gehlee each have their own dreams and journeys. For Cupids, part of the joy is watching their friendship grow, seeing how their personalities complement each other, and letting those moments unfold naturally.

Jeo Ong

A content creator introduced on PBB as “Ang Adventure Boy-Next-Door ng Palawan.” Viewers also know him through the OngFam family of vloggers.

Gehlee Dangca
